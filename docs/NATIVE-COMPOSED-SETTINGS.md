## Verification and limits

- [Sticky uncertainty](<NATIVE-SAVE-UNCERTAINTY.md>) blocks replacement saves until fresh lifetime after missing ACK; cannot cancel/rollback admitted writes. **Local evidence:** [later successful scale-1.25 regression](<../app/evidence/save-uncertainty-success-01/result.json>) does not qualify unknown-state paint/timeouts.
- 25-second in-app deadline requests shutdown. Runner: 40-second polling plus up to two seconds TERM/two seconds KILL/helper timeout—not universal wall-clock bound. Retained pidfds fence cleanup, including survivors after root exit; unknown containment never counts as success.
- Required proof: owned Wayland, live ACK, independent file/metadata, clean exit; app scale is not compositor fractional scaling.
- Unqualified: physical input/IME, real-key/account/model validity, clipboard erasure, arbitrary provider confinement, live approvals and parity. Wiping covers owned app/Core buffers only.
